About NAIMISH PHARMACY

NAIMISH PHARMACY is a free, independent educational resource built for B.Pharm students across India. Designed and maintained by Amarjeet Kashyap, the platform organizes study materials the way pharmacy students actually study — semester-wise, unit-wise, and always accessible.

The platform covers all eight semesters of the B.Pharm curriculum with subjects, units, notes, important questions, previous year questions (PYQs), multiple choice questions (MCQs) with explanations, practicals, and curated references.

Study tools include a progress tracker, streak system, bookmarks, flashcards, searchable PYQ library, MCQ engine, and offline-ready local storage — all designed to help students stay consistent and focused during exam preparation.

This is not affiliated with any university or regulatory body (PCI, AICTE, or any specific institution). All materials are meant to complement — not replace — your official syllabus, faculty lectures, and textbook reading.
